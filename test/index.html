        Tracking

        Basic adds Hit, Walk, HBP, K and Out buttons under the batter, and an At-bats page at the end. Full is for kid pitch and up: a Track page at the far left with singles through home runs, out types, runners scoring, plays in the field and a pitch counter.

        Track RBIs

        For kid pitch. Adds an RBI button: tap it once per run the batter drives in. It adds the run and credits the last batter. Use + on the Score page for runs on errors, steals or passed balls.
